Knowledge Base

Frequently asked inquiries regarding platform security and access.

How is my biometric data encrypted?
We utilize AES-256 military-grade encryption for all stored data. Biometric tokens are hashed client-side and never leave your device in their raw format, ensuring zero-knowledge architecture.
Can I access the portal from unregistered networks?
By default, external network access is blocked for Enterprise accounts unless explicitly permitted by your IT administrator through VPN tunneling or verified IP whitelisting.
What happens if I lose my Security Token?
You must immediately contact your IT desk to revoke the active token. A hardware replacement or temporary software authenticator will be issued upon identity verification.
